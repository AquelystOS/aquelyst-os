## 🌐 Set Up Web3Forms (10 minutes)

### 1. Create free account
- Go to https://web3forms.com
- Sign up (takes 2 minutes)

### 2. Get Access Key
- Log in to Web3Forms
- Copy your Access Key

### 3. Update HTML form
- Open `web3forms_template.html`
- Find: `value="YOUR_ACCESS_KEY_HERE"`
- Replace with your actual key
- Save

### 4. Add form to website
- Copy the form to AqueLyst.com (contact page)
- Or embed via page builder (Webflow, Wix, etc.)

### 5. Test it
- Fill out form on website
- Check your email (Web3Forms sends it)
- Copy the email body
- Paste in app → "Web3Forms Email Paste"
- Lead appears in database!
